# v2 results (in development on the `v2` branch)

The v2 edition (`--v2`): shared-vocabulary subjects, a fourth crossed
phrasing, natural-null controls beside the phantoms, three contradiction
shapes aimed at token differencing, answer-readable credit, capability
flags, and the additive score — `docs/scoring.md` and `docs/families.md`
say what each is. Worlds under `worlds/v2/` (the same five specs as v1's
100/500/1500 and 500 seeds 2–3, regenerated byte for byte by a golden test).
Only the in-process arms have been run, and only at 500 seed 1; nothing
here is a leaderboard yet.

**Environment.** One Apple-silicon laptop (macOS), CPU only, 2026-09-15,
Rust stable; `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` for every embedding arm, the engram
arm with `jina-reranker-v1-turbo-en` and `deberta-v3-small-tasksource-nli`
(engram-core at the revision `Cargo.toml` pins — the v1 stack, untouched).

```sh
cargo run --release --features fastembed -- --v2 --sizes 500 \
    --arms engram,rag,grep,curated,whole,chance,tfidf \
    --json results/v2/reference-arms/500-seed1.json
```

## The TF-IDF arm, v1 against v2

`results/v1/reference-arms/500-seed1-tfidf.json` is the same arm on the v1
500 world: **564 at 61% success**, signal share 0.82, 99 tokens per answer
— above the reference system's v1 511 at 85%, which is the crack the field
submission reported (823 at 76% on the 1500 world). On v2 the same arm
scores **576 at 44%**, 162 under the reference system's 738, and the
mechanisms it rode are each visible in a column:

- the idf oracle is dead — `phantom_fp` 1.00 (it was 0.00 on v1: every
  word of a v2 phantom subject was written somewhere) and `natural_fp`
  1.00;
- the crossed phrasing is unreachable — `crossed_r@5` 0.00, and the four
  phrasings weigh equally, so retrieval falls from 61% to 46%;
- `stale_above` 0.70: the stale sibling's shorter title wins cosine;
- the contradiction column keeps what token differencing can do (`value`
  and `unit`, tier-1 recall 0.38) and shows what it cannot: `reworded`
  and `clause` positives are missed and the `synonym` negative is not
  flagged, which is the trap working both ways.

What it still earns is honest: 100 token points (136 tokens per answer)
and 42 signal points, plus the families it attempts — temporal, deletion,
a fair share of drift. That is where the additive score puts a lexical
system: above the flat stores that show ten whole notes, well under a
memory that knows what it knows.

## What v2 did to the reference system

Its v2 number is not a v1 number regraded. Three columns moved, and each
is a finding about the product, not the bench:

1. **`crossed_r@5` 0.02** (rag 0.17): a question sharing no content word
   with its note is where the keyword channel and the reranker vote have
   nothing to hold, and the hybrid lands under pure cosine.
2. **`hedge` 0.40**: the calibrated "not in memory" line, fitted on v1's
   register, declines 40% of answerable v2 questions. On v2 a decline on
   an answerable question is zero signal, so the signal score is 34 where
   the retrieval focus alone would give about 60.
3. **Contradiction 46% (t1 0.04) and drift 47%** against 75% and 91% on
   v1: the title-NLI nomination gate barely raises the v2 `value` flips.
   The three-word lowercase subjects are the suspect — the gate's subject
   guard was tuned on capitalised coined names — and that is a product
   question for engram, recorded in its graph, not a v2 knob.

None of this is fixed here: the engram arm is the v1 stack at the pinned
revision, and the v2 branch measures it as it is.
